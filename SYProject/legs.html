Top Legs Exercises

Squats

Squats are a fundamental exercise for building strength in the legs and glutes.

Lunges

Lunges are excellent for targeting the quadriceps, hamstrings, and glutes.

Leg Press

The leg press machine is great for isolating the leg muscles and building strength.

Calf Raises

Calf raises are effective for strengthening the calf muscles.
